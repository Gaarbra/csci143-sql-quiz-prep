## Recursive CTEs and Quiz 3

DataLemur has no free recursive CTE problem, and LeetCode's are Premium. Use the recursive CTE explorer in `index.html` and rework `quiz2/recursive_cte.sql` from his repo by hand.

Quiz 3 (bytes per row) has no DataLemur or LeetCode equivalent. Use the byte calculator in `index.html`, then check yourself with `pg_column_size` in psql and `dbstat` in sqlite3.
